Deterministic Math

The 3-Layer Triangulation Model

Every candidate is scored using an objective, explainable mathematical composite formula:

CombinedRisk = (0.45 × Reconciliation) + (0.35 × Macro) + (0.20 × Micro)
Layer 1: Score Reconciliation (45% Weight)

Compares raw physical OMR bubble scans against the published server database marks.

Rule: Any positive jump $\Delta = (\text{Server} - \text{Raw}) \ge +10.0$ marks triggers an immediate statutory objection. Catches single-digit scores manually inflated to 50–55 marks.

Layer 2: Macro Centre Patterns (35% Weight)

Tests the entire score distribution of an exam centre against the state/national population baseline.

Statistical Test: Two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov (KS) test. If statistic $D > 0.35$ with $p < 0.01$, flags centre-wide leakage or mass broadcasting.

Layer 3: Micro Seating Proximity (20% Weight)

Identifies collusion between candidates seated at adjacent desks (Euclidean distance $d \le \sqrt{2}$).

Metric: Adapted Wollack $\omega$ index and Holland $K$-index. Flags pairs sharing $\ge 6$ identical wrong distractors ($z$-score $> 3\sigma$).

Legal Adjudication

Adjudication & Section 65B Dossiers

Non-Punitive Decision Support

Terra Trace does not automate penalties. Human officers review every case and select from 4 statutory statuses:

  • Pending: Under active forensic review.
  • Confirmed: Evidence verified against physical records; statutory notice issued.
  • False Positive: Discrepancy explained by official administrative correction (e.g. court grace marks).
  • Escalated: Referred to state cyber cell / police vigilance for hardware seizure.
Mandatory Justification: Officers cannot change a candidate's status without entering a logged written justification (e.g. "Physical OMR verified: raw score 3.0 vs server 53.0").
Tough Questions Defense

Defense Q&A for Judges

Q1: "Is this using Artificial Intelligence or Machine Learning?"

Answer: "No, and that is our strongest feature. In a court of law, 'black box' machine learning weights and LLM hallucinations are legally inadmissible. Terra Trace uses deterministic statistical forensics: exact item reconciliation, two-sample KS distribution tests, and Wollack $\omega$ seating proximity indices. Every score is explainable down to the exact math."

Q2: "Can an auditor tamper with evidence to frame a student?"

Answer: "No. Terra Trace uses an append-only cryptographic audit register. The instant raw files are uploaded, their SHA-256 block hashes are stamped immutably. Any manual change to an OMR CSV file breaks the hash and invalidates the Section 65B certificate immediately."

Q3: "What if an entire centre did well because they had great teachers?"

Answer: "The Macro layer does not flag centres for high average marks. It tests for unnatural distribution shapes—such as an artificial cluster strictly at the qualifying cutoff (50-55 marks) with zero room variance. Normal high-performing schools exhibit natural bell curves."

Q4: "How do you protect student privacy under the DPDP Act?"

Answer: "Terra Trace is designed to run air-gapped on on-premise government servers without internet access. Candidate names, phone numbers, and Aadhaar numbers are never ingested—only anonymized Roll IDs and Seat Coordinates."
